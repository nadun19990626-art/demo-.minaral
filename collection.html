Care & handling

Handle with intention.

Keep dry

Avoid prolonged exposure to water, steam and high humidity unless the product care sheet says otherwise.

Clean gently

Use a soft, dry cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners and harsh chemicals.

Store safely

Keep individual pieces protected from impact, scratches and direct heat.

Respect the finish

Do not polish, sand or chemically treat the surface unless specifically instructed for that material.
